## How the page is organised

- **Shell (v4)**: the top bar carries the brand, a **Client** picker (Cboe, Goldman Sachs, Alpha Client Demo), the area tabs Plan · Measure · Deliver · Team · Clients, search, theme, help, settings and the user. The sidebar shows Home, a **Line of business** picker for the chosen client, and only the area you are in; each page expands underneath into its own features (Datorama-style), so the page's tab strip lives in the sidebar. Switch on *Compare LOBs* per workspace in Settings → Clients & workspaces and the client gets an "All LOBs · combined" option plus a **Lines of business** page that puts every LOB side by side with the account total.
- **Density and contrast.** Spacing follows Datorama: 60px top bar, 284px sidebar with 12px-tall items, 36px page gutters, 26px card padding, 14px table rows, 14px base type and 31px page titles. The chrome (top bar, sidebar) is near-black against a lighter content canvas so the working area stands out; the light theme uses the same dark chrome over a warm grey canvas with white cards.
- **Look and feel**: a black top bar (brand, workspace picker, area tabs Plan · Measure · Deliver · Team · Clients, search, theme switch, help, settings, user), a warm dark sidebar listing every page under its area, a page header with breadcrumb and actions, and each page's own tabs in a strip under the title. Warm dark theme by default (amber accent on stone/charcoal), light theme from the sun/moon switch in the top bar; the choice is remembered per browser. Icons are inline SVG, no emoji anywhere in the UI. Help panels are collapsed by default and remember their state.
- **Navigation follows the data flow**: Home (a per-user dashboard) → the workspace picker (`Client - Line of Business`, a filter for every page) → Plan (Plans, Audiences, Datorama) → Measure (Campaigns, Reports, Reach & Frequency) → Deliver (Studio, Pivot, Landscape) → Team (Calendar, Team, Playbook). Cross-client settings sit behind the gear: clients & workspaces, users, publishers, benchmarks, rates, templates (report catalogue, naming), library. ⌘K searches everything.
- **Each plan has a Strategy tab** answering the nine pre-launch questions (R&F needed? infrastructure sufficient? audience? rates on file? report templates? vendor request template? questions per publisher? timeline? schedule?) with status and links.
- **Landscape** aggregates observations, benchmarks and rates across workspaces by publisher × channel with the guards that decide what may be blended (consent level, same threshold, same geography, n ≥ 3, medians never means).
- A demo workspace, `Alpha Client Demo - Demo`, carries a plan spanning DV360, social, site direct, newsletter, OOH, linear TV and CTV for testing.
- **Access.** Sign-in is the Claude account. A signed-in viewer without a Hub profile sees the Create your profile screen (Datorama-style sign-in card), submits a request, and sees Waiting for approval until an admin reviews it under Settings → Users → Access requests and grants role, workspaces and capabilities; the person does not need to be online or reach the admin. Admins can pause an account or Reset sign-in (clears the account link and mints a new invite link). Settings → Security records the password policy (10+ characters, letters and numbers, one special character, 180-day rotation) and the rotation status per user; on claude.ai that policy is informational because passwords belong to the Claude account, and `hosting/` holds the runbook, schema and adapter contract for a self-hosted build where an identity provider enforces it and any visitor can register at your own domain.
- **Activity log.** Every create, update, delete and restore (with the record before and after), plus sign-ins, access requests and decisions, page views and exports, each stamped with the profile that did it; Settings → Activity log filters by profile, action and collection and exports to a workbook.
- **Section dashboards.** Plan, Measure, Deliver and Team each open on a per-profile dashboard: first visit asks for three or more widgets or the default (task tracker on top, then one widget per page in the area); Customize adds, moves, resizes, configures or resets widgets from two catalogues (16 work widgets: tasks, live campaigns, flight calendar, report schedule, quick actions, quick links, open questions, stream freshness, taxonomy compliance, QA status, changes, documents, team load, lines of business, summary strip, rich text box; 12 measurement widgets: pacing table by cut and granularity, scrollable media plan, spend by month, reach curve, forecast KPIs, latest version change, audience sizes, benchmarks, deduplication, rates, activity feed, budget vs approved). Help panels are now hover ? marks beside page titles.
- **Sign-in is claude.ai's.** There is no separate Hub username or password. A person invited in Settings → Users receives a link carrying an invite code; on first open the Hub links their Claude account to their Hub profile and walks them through profile and home-dashboard setup.
- **Datorama tab** turns the media plan in a workspace into a Datorama workspace plan: normalised plan lines with stable IDs, a question queue for anything the client profile cannot resolve, a taxonomy builder that blocks volatile attributes from names, generated trafficking sheet / classifications / manifests, a ten-check compliance engine reported as % of spend, a stream map with one authoritative stream per metric, delivery ingest with run-rate forecasting and month close (three spend metrics), the benchmark upload loop, upload-ready stream files with email-in subjects and freshness alarms, the seven-step diagnostic ladder, and the 19-step analyst runbook.
- **R&F inputs (per plan)** replaces the old Reconcile tab: a monthly or weekly grid of the yellow cells from the In-Flight Reporting Template (impressions and reach per partner per period, flight-to-date reach with the workbook CHECK rule, the one DCM pull), the dedup-rate table (divisor per period, rolling, vs the 1.40 floor, CHECK when below 1), the Sainsbury union table and per-partner duplication / turnover. Each partner row shows which source it is read from (CM360 per-site, the CM360 pull, DV360, LinkedIn, Meta, Reddit, newsletter 1P send report, vendor 1P, Comscore) with how to pull it. Numbers come in by hand OR from an export dropped in Campaigns → Inbox: the source's field map recognises the columns, the analyst can remap them, apply the rows to the grid, and save the mapping so the next file from that vendor is read the same way. Reach is never summed across daily rows (flagged as a conflict); multi-issue newsletter periods enter the largest issue as a floor.
- **Dedup** (Measure group) is the deduplication bank: every CM360 unique-reach pull, overlap interpretation and measured divisor kept over time, across publishers (divisor with its window and pull date) and within publisher per audience (duplicate share, turnover multiple); CM360 reach reports are registered here against their schedule.
- **Settings → Rates** is a navigator: at agency level cut by publisher → channel → audience → product → funnel → client → LOB → plan; inside a workspace by plan → publisher → channel → audience → product → funnel. Click a chip to reorder the cuts, click a row to drill, the breadcrumb goes back up. Records are rate lines, register values, forecasts they were applied in (linked to the projection), agency defaults, benchmarks and observations, each with tier and source. The rate history and cards sit below it.
- **Multi-region clients (Cboe).** Uploading the Cboe flowchart workbook (RAW DATA from Prisma + manual EMEA/APAC rows, Reference Sheet, Approved ATPs) in Plans creates one workspace per line of business (Cboe - Brand, Retail, Institutional Derivatives, Data Vantage), keeps every row's buying region and team, stores the Reference Sheet as a classification registry and the ATPs as approved budgets, and adds a **Regions** view to the Datorama tab: per-region cards (US/Prisma positional names vs EMEA/APAC lookup-only names, classification coverage, currencies, last actualised month), the shared 15-attribute schema with where each attribute comes from per region, LOB totals together or by region with an explicit through-month against the ATP, and row checks (unclassified names, months not summing to net, fee-rule mismatches, duplicate registry keys). These clients carry `needs_rf: false`, so the reach and frequency tabs are hidden for them.
- **Workspace documentation.** Guides, runbooks and reference notes stored against a workspace appear under Datorama → Docs (rendered from Markdown, checklists remembered per week per browser) and are searchable; a troubleshooting guide with "Symptom" sections is surfaced on the Diagnostics page. The Goldman Sachs - Corporate Brand workspace carries the weekly QA guides, the misclassification troubleshooting guide, the Datorama profile breakdown, the SA360 mapping explanation and the conversion-tag guide (sources in `platform/corp-brand/`), plus its 78-stream Datorama inventory in Streams.
- **Live Datorama infrastructure.** From the Corp Brand and PWM infrastructure workbooks, each Goldman Sachs workspace carries its data load rules (action, metrics, window, filters, streams applied to) with an **expiry calendar** on the Streams page: a suppression rule with an end date lapses on that date and lets rows back in with no code change, so lapsed and soon-to-lapse rules are flagged. The Taxonomy page lists the live name-parsing patterns (dimension read, streams touched, positions extracted; account-wide patterns with no stream filter are flagged because a naming change for another line of business moves this workspace's numbers) and the classification entities grouped by concept, flagging concepts that have parallel lookup entities nothing keeps in sync. Stream records were enriched with channel, purpose, load rules and scheduled end dates.
- **Trackers and process documents** (campaign launch tracker, PWM reporting process, reporting deliverables timeline, IAS reporting tracker, insights training canvas, CB analytics project tracker, audience tracker, floodlight structure, site-direct partner contacts) are stored as workspace documents under Datorama → Docs. They are text, not Hub tasks: their owners are not Hub users, so nothing is assigned from them automatically.
- **Plan versions.** Every upload for a campaign becomes the next version and the primary plan automatically; earlier versions stay with their forecasts, registers and change log. The plan's **Versions** tab shows the timeline, compares any two versions (cost, impressions, forecast reach and frequency, effective CPM; by publisher, channel, market, region, LOB, audience, month, or line-level events) and exports the comparison as a workbook, CSV, PNG or a rich-text table for slides.
- **Forecast with live rates.** Under a saved forecast's table, every publisher line lists the on-target rate, frequency, device ratio and pool it was saved with; editing any of them recomputes the projection at once, per market, and the result can be saved as a new forecast. **Portfolio R&F** (plans whose lines carry a LOB; theoretical on Alpha Client Demo) shows reach by LOB × market, by LOB, and the whole campaign de-duplicated, with cross-LOB duplication.
- **Audiences** is segment-first: pick a segment, see the projections per publisher from the latest saved forecast, the rate each line rests on (publisher-provided, or the channel benchmark from the default-value library, labelled with tier and source), and every publisher that gave an audience size.
- **Cboe brief (28 Sep 2026).** `platform/cboe/operations-manual-and-web-app-data-contract-2026-09-28.md` is the operations manual and web-app data contract; it is also stored as a workspace document. Implemented in the Hub: the stream map with `authoritative_for`, `finalization_window_days`, `reprocess_cadence` and `ingest_route`; the 16-token media buy template; classification and pattern inventories; region process notes; and **QA runs** — drop a Datorama Table export, the site validates it (§5.2), runs the four tiers (§5.3), reports PASSED / BLOCKED / PASSED WITH FLAGS / FAILED RECONCILIATION, stores accepted variances and overrides with author and reason, and generates the flowchart workbook (four LOB tabs plus Overview & Pacing) as DRAFT or FINAL. Workspaces of one client can share Datorama records through a `group` on the client row.
- **Layout details borrowed from Datorama.** Hover ? markers explain fields, thresholds and metrics; first-time upload pages show a large drag-and-drop area with a Browse button; QA runs carry a stepper (Validate → Tiers → Reconcile → Flowchart); decisions such as the B6 rule are option cards.
- **Prompts, toolbars and data housing.** Search and filter boxes carry a magnifier and italic prompt text; page and card toolbars put the title left and quiet actions right; multi-step processes (QA runs) end in a sticky footer with Cancel, a step indicator and the primary action; an open plan shows its name in a title box with a Version history chip; drawers open with a breadcrumb (Data streams › Edit stream). Every table gets column-type glyphs (abc · 123 · date), click-to-sort headers, hover rows and a "1 – N of N items" footer.
- **Admin patterns.** The user chip in the top bar opens an account menu (name, email, role, quick links, database usage bar). Settings → Users is a searchable table with a Create new drawer, status switches and workspace chips; settings forms use a label-left layout with section headers and required marks.
- **Controls.** Forms use labelled fields, segmented controls, switches, drop zones and a side drawer (streams are edited in one); question-queue cards are structured decisions.
- **Library** holds the default-value library (every row with source, vintage, status and the question it answers), the fallback chains, policy blocks and the source registry.

## Working on it

The page has no build step. To test headlessly:

```sh
npm install jsdom xlsx
node --check <(sed -n '/<script>/,/<\/script><\/body>/p' app/analytics-hub.html | sed '1s/^<script>//;$s/<\/script><\/body><\/html>$//')
```

Storage caveats worth knowing before changing the data model: records already in the page's database keep their shape, so field renames need a migration path; the database is per-artifact, so publishing to a new URL starts empty.

- **Section widgets.** Each area's dashboard picker leads with widgets built for that stage of the process: Plan (campaign stage tracker, plan pipeline, flight notices, rate outliers, audience coverage, version timeline, plan hygiene, budget split), Measure (campaign scoreboard, pacing gauges, spend burn, delivery anomalies, variance breakout, QA history, reports due, freshness matrix), Deliver (deliverables tracker, headline KPIs, report calendar, closeout progress, recent exports, landscape snapshot) and Team (my tasks, priority matrix, capacity grid, week ahead, needs-a-decision box, decisions log, handoffs). Every widget respects the client and LOB pickers, and the general widgets stay available under the suggested ones.
- **Data streams (intent-first uploads).** Measure → Reports opens on *Data streams*, and a *Data streams* page sits in the Plan, Measure and Deliver sidebars. A file is built up in stages, the way a Datorama data stream is: **Intent** (QA a report, interpret reach & overlap, delivery/pacing, R&F inputs, benchmarks, placement facts, media plan version, reference document) → **File** (drop it, or pick an upload already on file) → **Recognise** → **Map** → **Process**. Recognition is client-wide and has three outcomes: a file whose columns match a template saved for the client runs on its own with the template's mapping and settings; a recognised source with new or missing columns (a template that covers most of the columns, or a platform whose column signature fired) opens the mapping with suggestions to confirm and the new fields highlighted; an unknown source asks which platform or exporter it is (or a new name) and then suggests a mapping from the dictionary. Every confirmed mapping is saved as a versioned client-wide template (optionally shared with every client), so the next file from that exporter processes itself. Each intent reuses the Hub's existing engine: the starter QA suite or the four-tier Datorama Table QA, the overlap interpreter (Comscore (000) columns scaled, exclusive/duplicate derived when one is missing), stream delivery ingest, R&F actuals, benchmark proposals (still confirmed by hand), placement facts (validated against the taxonomy, with a facts template), plan-workbook ingest and the document library. The **Platform dictionary** lists, per platform, what the export carries, which Hub field it maps to and where that is documented; see the sources table below.

- **Top bar.** The brand is the words "Analytics Hub" alone; the active area tab (Plan, Measure, Deliver, Team) is a filled tile in that area's colour.
- **Dashboard layout engine.** Section dashboards sit on a free 12-column grid: in Customize mode drag a widget by its title bar to move it, pull its right edge, bottom edge or corner to resize it in either or both directions, and the other widgets slide out of the way and pack upward with animated transitions; Fit sets a widget's height to its content, ↑/↓ reorder in reading order, and every change saves per dashboard as you go. Existing half/full-width widgets migrate to grid units on first open.
- **Active reports (Reports tab).** One tab per client: name a report, set its cadence (weekly with a weekday, monthly with a day of month, or daily on business days), send time, time zone, owner, line of business, optional platform and report type, recipients and notes. It lands in the weekly, monthly or daily list with its next date, and on a calendar that toggles between This month and This week (previous/next/today), laid out on a fluid seven-column grid. Reports are stored as report rules, so the Team tab's outreach → QA → send tasks are generated for the next three months and the existing schedule widgets keep working; Pause keeps a report on file without dates.
- **QA a report / Interpret a report landings.** Both start from platform tiles; a platform opens tiles of the report types templatized for it (with the mappings saved for the client), and choosing one opens two tiles side by side — Upload a report (drag-drop or browse, run through the data-stream wizard with the platform preset) and View old reports (earlier interpretations, QA results and runs for that platform) — with the report type's field map underneath (export column → Hub field → meaning → whether the name is verified, and whether it is present in the file just dropped). Report types are named from the CM360 and DV360 API discovery documents and the Meta SDK levels where those were reachable; the rest are labelled unverified.
- **Create user.** Settings → Users → Create new opens a wide, horizontal form: Email, First name, Last name, Role, Client, Lines of business (all, or a selection that follows the chosen client) and a live welcome-email preview.
